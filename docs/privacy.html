Privacy Policy

IndicPhotoOCR (Android app, package io.github.asadalk.indicphotoocr)

Last updated: 7 November 2026

IndicPhotoOCR is created by people at the Vision, Language and Learning Group (VL2G), Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur. We built the app so that your photos stay on your phone.

1. Everything happens on your phone. Text is found and read by models that run on your device. The app does not have internet permission, so it cannot send your photos, the recognised text or anything else to us or to anyone else.

2. What we collect: nothing. The app collects no personal information, no photos, no recognised text, no location, no contacts, no device identifiers, no analytics and no crash reports. There are no advertisements and no third-party tracking or advertising libraries.

3. Photos. A photo you take with the camera is saved only in the app's private temporary folder so that it can be read, and is replaced by the next photo. A photo you pick from your gallery or share to the app is only read, never copied or changed. Android removes the app's data when you uninstall it.

4. Recognised text. The text is shown on the screen only. It leaves the app only if you choose Copy or Share yourself, and then it goes where you send it.

5. Permissions. The app asks for no special permissions. Photos come from your phone's own camera app or photo picker, which you control.

6. Settings stored on the phone. The app keeps one small setting on your phone: which processor (CPU, GPU or NPU) is fastest on it. It contains no personal information.

7. Children. The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

8. Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be shown here with a new date.

9. Contact. Questions: the issues page of the source code repository.
