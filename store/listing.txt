================================================================
----------------------------------------------------------------
IndicPhotoOCR 2.0 reads text in photos of signboards, shop fronts, notices, labels and pages, in English and 11 Indian languages: Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam.

Fully offline and private
• Everything runs on your phone. The app has no internet permission, so your photos and text never leave your device.
• No account, no ads, no tracking, no data collected.

Simple to use
• Take a photo, pick one from your gallery, or share an image to the app from any other app.
• Every word found is marked with a box on the photo, and the recognised text is shown below.
• Copy or share the recognised text with one tap.
• Automatically uses the fastest processor on your phone (CPU, GPU or NPU).

Open source
IndicPhotoOCR 2.0 is free software under the AGPL-3.0 licence. Source code: https://github.com/Bhashini-IITJ/IndicPhotoOCR/tree/android

Developed by people at the Vision, Language and Learning Group (VL2G), IIT Jodhpur. Supported by Bhashini.

Note: recognition can be wrong for very small, blurred, curved, handwritten or decorative text. Please check important text yourself.
